Privacy Policy for the OSRS Wiki App (FOSS)

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy describes the unofficial OSRS Wiki Android app’s FOSS / F-Droid distribution (the foss flavor of com.omiyawaki.osrswiki). It does not describe the Google Play build.

This FOSS build does not include a crash-metrics SDK, Google Play Billing, or Google Play Services in-app purchases.

1. Information on your device

The app stores data locally so it can work as a wiki reader:

  • Search history and recently viewed pages, until you clear them or uninstall the app
  • Saved / offline articles and cached wiki assets, until you clear offline storage or uninstall
  • Map tiles shipped with the app as local MBTiles. The map tab does not download a remote map-tile service

The app does not keep voice recordings.

2. Wiki content

Search queries, article HTML, images, and related wiki assets are requested over HTTPS from oldschool.runescape.wiki (the official Old School RuneScape Wiki).

Those HTTP requests send a User-Agent that includes the app version, Android version, device manufacturer, and device model. Wiki article JavaScript may also request additional wiki or community hosts needed to render a page.

3. Optional feedback

If you submit Report an Issue or Request a Feature, the app sends your title and description, plus app version, distribution flavor (foss), Android version, and device model (manufacturer, model, brand, and product) to a Cloudflare Worker at https://osrswiki-feedback.omiyawaki.workers.dev/. The Worker creates a GitHub Issue. The app does not embed a GitHub API token. If you do not use feedback, this data is not sent.

4. Grand Exchange charts

Item price charts request timeseries data from https://prices.runescape.wiki.

5. Videos in wiki articles

When an article includes a video, preview thumbnails load from https://i.ytimg.com. Playing a video can load a YouTube embed (youtube.com or youtube-nocookie.com) or open a YouTube app on the device.

6. Optional voice search

Voice search uses Android’s system SpeechRecognizer (SpeechRecognizer.createSpeechRecognizer). This app does not bundle a speech SDK. On many devices the platform default is Google’s online recognizer, so audio is sent over the network to that provider. Other devices may use a different vendor engine or on-device recognition. Audio is used only to produce a search query and is not stored by the app. You can skip voice search and revoke microphone permission in system settings.

7. Optional support links

Donate opens GitHub Sponsors in a browser. A separate outbound link can open the wiki’s Patreon page. This FOSS build does not include Google Play Billing and has no in-app purchases.

8. What this FOSS build does not do

  • It does not include a crash-metrics SDK or send automatic crash telemetry
  • It does not include Google Play Billing or Google Play Services in-app purchases
  • It does not require an account

9. Your choices

  • Voice search is optional; microphone access can be revoked in device settings
  • Feedback is optional
  • Cached and offline content can be cleared in app settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data

10. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The wiki content is about a video game rated for teen audiences. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information through feedback, contact us through the in-app feedback form.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when the FOSS build’s data flows change. Significant changes will be included in app updates.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the app’s feedback form (More → Feedback). That path uses the Cloudflare Worker described above.
